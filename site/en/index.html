Services

We usually start with an audit. Afterwards you know what state the application is in and can decide what to do next – with me or without me.

1

Application audit

I review the code, architecture, dependencies, database and deployment. You get a written report: what is on fire, what can wait and how much the modernization will take. Scope and price are agreed up front.

2

Step-by-step modernization

From .NET Framework, WCF or WebForms to current .NET in small steps, without a big rewrite. Every step is backed by automated tests, the application keeps running the whole time and users barely notice.

3

Development and maintenance

New features, fixes and taking over code from someone who has left. Happy to work on a part-time or fixed-term contract, on my own or as part of your team.
